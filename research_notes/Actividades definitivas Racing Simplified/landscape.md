# Landscape of interactive F1 and motorsport fan experiences (as of October 2026)

Research note: 15 search or fetch calls. Many traction figures available on the open web are old (2021 to 2024) or self-reported. Each one is dated below. Anything I could not source is under Gaps, not presented as fact.

## 1. Which F1 web games and interactives exist, how do they play, and which have measurable traction?

### Takeaway
The F1 interactive space has three layers. The first is official season-long games: F1 Fantasy is the only one with traction in the millions. The second is fan-built "second screen" data tools: MultiViewer, f1-dash, f1-race-replay and Box Box Club. These have real but niche audiences, built mostly by word of mouth among hardcore fans. The third is a long tail of small clones: F1 Wordle-likes, lights-out reaction tests and pit-stop toys, with no verifiable traction. I found no F1 interactive with a documented viral moment comparable to Wordle, Immaculate Grid or Poeltl.

### Cited Findings

**Official season-long games**
- F1 Fantasy (official, free). About 1.5 million people signed up for the 2024 season, including a record number of US players after a 25% year-on-year rise in the US. Source: an article published before the 2025 season. — [Speedway Digest / F1 release](https://speedwaydigest.com/index.php/news/racing-news/426952-f1-fantasy-returns-for-the-sports-75th-anniversary-following-a-bumper-2024/)
- PlanetF1 reported "over two million users" signed up in 2024. This conflicts with the 1.5M figure; the difference may be users versus teams. — [PlanetF1](https://www.planetf1.com/news/daniel-ricciardo-price-f1-fantasy)
- F1 Fantasy grew from about 1.6M teams (2022) to more than 2.4M teams by Abu Dhabi 2023. Beating about 2.5M teams was the benchmark for the 2024 Global League. — [SI / F1 Briefings](https://www.si.com/fannation/racing/f1briefings/news/2024-f1-fantasy-your-ultimate-guide-to-beating-formula-ones-global-league)
- One unsourced market-stats page claims more than 10M registered F1 Fantasy players in 2025. It conflicts with the official figures and is unreliable. — [aleaitsolutions](https://www.aleaitsolutions.com/fantasy-sports-app-market-statistics/)
- About 1 million players were affected by a single crash in Hungary in 2021 (old data). — [grandprix.com.au](https://www.grandprix.com.au/fan-zone/news/winners-and-losers-a-mid-season-f1-fantasy-review)
- Mechanics: a $100M virtual budget for drivers and constructors, 2 transfers per race, prices driven by a 3-race rolling performance window, and leagues. — [GP247](https://www.grandprix247.com/formula-1-news/join-the-gp247-f1-fantasy-league-this-season)
- F1 Fantasy publishes a season "Wrapped" recap with in-numbers stats and an interview with the global winner, the Spotify Wrapped pattern applied to fantasy. — [Formula1.com, 2022](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/f1-fantasy-wrapped-the-2022-season-in-numbers-and-your-global-winner.1MlV0Jy9G26zpxL0Dl4cPU)
- An ecosystem of third-party companion tools has grown around F1 Fantasy, for example boxboxf1fantasy.com with points and price trackers for 2026, and "points per million" strategy blogs. This is evidence of deep engagement among a core of players. — [Box Box F1 Fantasy stats](https://boxboxf1fantasy.com/stats/); [Into the Chicane](https://intothechicane.com/2026/02/28/f1-fantasy-stop-picking-drivers-start-picking-value-the-ppm-strategy-that-wins-f1-fantasy-in-2026/)
- GridRival (independent fantasy, launched 2021): 180,000 registered users by March 2022 (old data). — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/motorsport-fantasy-gaming-platform-gridrival-announces-180-000-registered-users-301510495.html)
- GridRival has about 200K Android installs according to an app tracker (a proxy, not active users). — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/gridrival-fantasy-racing/com.gridrival.gr_app)
- The Race ran a promoted comparison between GridRival and F1 Fantasy. — [The Race](https://www.the-race.com/promoted/fantasy-f1-game-showdown-gridrival-vs-fantasy-f1/)

**Official and team apps (2025–2026)**
- The official F1 app's "Race Experience" (built by Globant) offers schedules, personalised timetables and navigation for people at the circuit. More than 1.2M fans attending Grands Prix used it during the 2026 season. It is a logistics tool, not a game. — [FutureFive](https://futurefive.co.nz/story/formula-1-app-adds-race-experience-for-grand-prix-fans)
- The Scuderia Ferrari app (IBM watsonx, update of May 2026) adds:
  - an AI Companion that answers questions using Ferrari race data
  - a Game Center with race predictors, quizzes, a timed Countdown Quiz, global leaderboards, badges and achievements
  - a "Race Timeline" that records the key moments of each session: pit stops, fastest laps, weather, VSC

  The 2025 version had daily fan polls and fan messages featured on Ferrari's social channels. — [IBM Newsroom, May 2026](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-01-IBM-Debuts-New-AI-Powered-Features-for-the-Scuderia-Ferrari-App); [PR Newswire, Oct 2026 US GP expansion](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ibm-and-scuderia-ferrari-hp-expand-ai-powered-fan-experience-ahead-of-the-united-states-grand-prix-302916255.html); [IBM 2025](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2025-05-01-ibm-and-scuderia-ferrari-hp-debut-reimagined-mobile-app-to-supercharge-global-formula-1-fan-experience)
- Coverage frames the Ferrari push as aimed at "younger and newer" viewers. — [AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/ai-applications/ibm-ferrari-hp-unveil-formula-1-fan-engagement-app)

**Fan-built data and second-screen tools**
- MultiViewer for F1 is a fan-built desktop client that requires a paid F1 TV subscription. Its creator estimated more than 40,000 users for the 2023 Bahrain opener, up from 10–15K the year before. The headline says "so popular even F1 teams use it" (old data, about 2023). — [iMore](https://www.imore.com/music-movies-tv/multiviewer-for-f1-the-fan-built-formula-1-app-so-popular-even-f1-teams-use-it)
- f1-dash.com is a free, open-source, unofficial real-time timing dashboard started in 2023 as a hobby project. It shows the leaderboard, tyres, gaps, laps and mini-sectors, and v3 is in progress for more customisation. No traffic numbers found. — [f1-dash.com](https://f1-dash.com/); [Lemmy dev post](https://lemmy.dbzer0.com/post/2508677/2416661)
- f1-race-replay (IAmTomShaw, Python with the Arcade game engine and FastF1) is an interactive race visualisation and replay tool with about 6.0K GitHub stars and +286 in 30 days, last pushed in June 2026. It is a desktop and developer tool, not a web product for casual fans. — [GitTrend](https://gittrend.io/repo/IAmTomShaw/f1-race-replay); [author's Substack](https://tomshaw.substack.com/p/how-im-analysing-f1-telemetry-using); [Starlog](https://starlog.is/articles/developer-tools/4f4d-f1-race-replay)
- Box Box Club: Formula Widgets (ARKADE CLUB, unofficial) offers home-screen, lock-screen and Apple Watch widgets, live timing, head-to-head driver comparisons, schedules with weather, F1, F2, F3 and F1 Academy coverage, and "community race ratings". Its store pages claim "3 million+ racing fans", but the US listing shows only about 260 reviews (4.8★). The claim is self-reported. — [App Store DE](https://apps.apple.com/DE/app/id1621241916); [App Store KR](https://apps.apple.com/KR/app/id1621241916); [APKMirror 5.4.12](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/arkade-club-pvt-ltd/box-box-club-formula-widgets/)
- The curated list "awesome-f1" on GitHub collects the fan data-tool ecosystem. — [awesome-f1](https://github.com/subinium/awesome-f1)

**Long tail: Wordle-likes, reaction tests**
- Sportsdle runs an F1 daily "Wordle" mode, an F1 immaculate grid, unlimited play, and a "challenge maker". In the challenge maker a creator picks a driver and sends a link, and the creator then sees who solved it and in how many tries. Clues compare team, country, age and titles, with green for an exact match, amber for close and arrows giving direction. The claims come from the site itself. — [Sportsdle F1 challenge maker](https://sportsdle.com/f1/wordle-maker); [Sportsdle blog](https://sportsdle.com/blog/best-f1-wordle-games)
- F1 "lights out" reaction tests: ReactionTimeTest.info (F1 mode, compares against the human median, history stored locally), the LightsOut: Reflex Test app, fdaytalk.com (benchmark of 200–300 ms) and App Store reflex apps. No traction or virality evidence found, and several results were spam mirrors. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/reactiontimetest); [RAWG](https://rawg.io/games/lightsout-reflex-test); [fdaytalk](https://www.fdaytalk.com/apps/f1-reaction-time-test/)
- Formula 1's official account joined the Wordle trend in January 2022 (brand participation, not a product). — [Campaign Live](https://www.campaignlive.com/article/everyone-talking-wordle-even-11-brands/1737281)

### Inferences
- F1 Fantasy is the only F1 interactive with proven mass scale: about 1.5–2M+ people. Its loop is weekly and season-long, built on pre-race decisions. It rewards hardcore fans, has a steep rules burden, and does almost nothing to explain what actually happened in the race.
- Fan data tools such as MultiViewer, f1-dash and f1-race-replay show that real timing data attracts devoted users. Their interface is expert-grade: tables, gaps, telemetry. None of them translates the data for a casual fan or turns it into a game.
- Box Box Club's "community race ratings" and Ferrari's "Race Timeline" are the closest things to a post-race layer. Both are thin: a rating, or a list of events. Neither has a narrative or a game.
- F1 Wordle-likes and reaction tests are commodities. They have no barrier to entry and no evidence of breakout. Replicating them would not differentiate Racing Simplified.
- Ferrari's 2026 move to predictors, quizzes and an AI Companion suggests teams see prediction and quiz loops as the default engagement tool. That makes "just predictions" crowded territory.

### Gaps
- No official F1 Fantasy player count for 2025 or 2026 was found. The most recent reliable figure is 2024.
- No official "F1 Predict" game could be confirmed in the searches. Its existence, launch date and traction remain unverified.
- No traffic figures found for f1-dash, TracingInsights, F1dle or any F1 Wordle-like. I found no searchable F1 Manager-style browser game with traction data.
- No interactive features of F1 TV (beyond MultiViewer's dependence on it) were documented in the searches.
- No current MultiViewer user numbers. The 40K figure dates from about 2023.
- No r/formula1 threads with engagement numbers were retrieved, because the search tool did not surface Reddit directly.

## 2. Beyond F1: the best interactive sports experiences, and the mechanics that made them spread

### Takeaway
The breakout sports interactives (Immaculate Grid, Poeltl, the Wordle lineage) share one pattern. They are a single daily puzzle that everyone plays, take minutes to finish, and produce a result that can be shared without spoilers, plus a hook that ranks you against everyone else, such as the rarity score. They spread through Reddit and Twitter posts by players, not marketing. They were then bought or legitimised by an institution: Sports Reference, the NBPA, and the NYT for Wordle. Journalism interactives such as the NYT "You Draw It" add a different mechanic: predict first, then the real data is revealed, then you see how other readers guessed.

### Cited Findings
- Immaculate Grid launched on 4 April 2023, created by Brian Minter, a software developer in Atlanta. It is a 3x3 grid with 9 guesses. Each square must satisfy a row criterion and a column criterion (team, stat or award), players cannot be reused, and a "rarity score" was added later to reward obscure answers. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculate_Grid)
- How it spread: in May 2023 the audience was small (friends). A friend posted it on Reddit, and the Twitter account @FoolishBB shared it on 13 June 2023. By July 2023 it averaged about 200,000 players per weekday, and it is popular with current and former MLB players. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculate_Grid)
- Sports Reference acquired it on 11 July 2023 and linked each square to answer lists on Baseball-Reference, creating a funnel into the database. NFL (20 July), NBA (25 July), NHL (26 July) and soccer "Immaculate Footy" (18 August 2023) versions followed. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculate_Grid); [Sports Reference blog](https://www.sports-reference.com/blog/2023/07/sports-reference-acquires-immaculate-grid/); [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/new-owner-of-immaculate-grid-baseball-game-eyes-football-other-sports/)
- Immaculate Grid is being turned into a TV game show by Religion of Sports, the company of Tom Brady and Michael Strahan. — [PhillyVoice](https://www.phillyvoice.com/immaculate-grid-tv-game-show-trivia-sports-reference-baseball-brady/)
- Poeltl (NBA, Gabe Danon) launched in February 2022 and drew about 240,000 visitors the day after launch. It gives 8 guesses at a mystery player, with attribute clues: team, conference, division, position, height, age and number. — [NBPA](https://nbpa.com/news/nbpa-jakob-poeltl-and-gabe-danon-come-together-to-relaunch-online-guessing-game-poeltl); [Uproxx](https://uproxx.com/dimemag/nba-version-wordle-poeltl/); [Pounding The Rock](https://www.poundingtherock.com/2022/2/25/22951271/jakob-poeltl-has-become-an-nba-trivia-game)
- The NBPA relaunched Poeltl in February 2024, with Jakob Poeltl as co-owner and ambassador. This is an example of a league or players' body legitimising a fan game. — [NBPA](https://nbpa.com/news/nbpa-jakob-poeltl-and-gabe-danon-come-together-to-relaunch-online-guessing-game-poeltl)
- Earlier sports Wordle clones such as Weddle (NFL) and WARdle (MLB) came before Poeltl. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/pop-culture/how-play-poeltl-everything-know-nba-word-guessing-game)
- Wordle's viral mechanic: a player began tweeting results as coloured emoji squares that did not spoil the answer. This led to the share button, which works as competitive status and free advertising. — [Silicon Republic](https://siliconrepublic.com/?p=957516); [The Conversation](https://theconversation.Com/wordle-has-been-hacked-but-thats-not-going-to-ruin-the-fun-175675)
- NYT "You Draw It": readers draw their guess of a trendline, then the real data is revealed, then they see other readers' guesses. This forces them to commit to a belief before the reveal. The series started around 2015–2017 (old, but still the canonical pattern). — [Distill, "Communicating with Interactive Articles"](https://distill.pub/2020/communicating-with-interactive-articles/); [blog summary](http://notawfulandboring.blogspot.com/2017/04/nyts-you-draw-it-series.html)
- The Pudding (a data-journalism publisher) and Quartz's "How do you draw a circle?" are cited as popular visual stories that use participation followed by a reveal. — [Distill](https://distill.pub/2020/communicating-with-interactive-articles/); [Wikipedia, The Pudding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pudding)

### Inferences
- The mechanics that spread, ranked by evidence:
  1. A single shared daily puzzle, which gives everyone a common conversation.
  2. A spoiler-free shareable result card or emoji grid.
  3. A comparative score against all players, for example rarity ("only 2% of players picked this").
  4. A session of a few minutes.
  5. Spread seeded by players on Reddit and Twitter rather than by marketing.
- Predict first, then reveal the real data, then compare with the crowd (the NYT model) fits a product built on real timing data. "Guess the gap after lap 30" or "draw how the race played out" becomes a learning moment, not trivia.
- The Immaculate Grid acquisition shows the game works as a funnel into a database (Baseball-Reference). Racing Simplified could equally turn each answer into a door into the race recap.

### Gaps
- No current (2025–2026) daily-player figures for Immaculate Grid or Poeltl were found. Footle was not researched in depth.
- No sports-specific NYT, The Athletic, ESPN or The Pudding interactive with published engagement metrics was found. The evidence for "You Draw It" is about the format, not traffic.
- NBA, NFL and soccer prediction games such as ESPN Tournament Challenge and Premier League Predictor were not covered within the call budget.

## 3. What do they all lack that a post-race, data-truthful, casual-fan product could own?

### Takeaway
The evidence shows a clear gap. Interactives with mass reach are either before the race (fantasy, predictors) or have nothing to do with a specific race (Wordle-likes, reaction tests). Data-truthful tools exist, but they are live, expert-grade dashboards. Nobody combines all four of these: post-race timing, casual-friendly play, a shared daily puzzle, and a spoiler-free shareable result.

### Cited Findings
- F1 Fantasy is decided before the race, with transfers and budget, and its season recap is a "Wrapped" of numbers, not an explanation of the race. — [GP247](https://www.grandprix247.com/formula-1-news/join-the-gp247-f1-fantasy-league-this-season); [Formula1.com Wrapped](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/f1-fantasy-wrapped-the-2022-season-in-numbers-and-your-global-winner.1MlV0Jy9G26zpxL0Dl4cPU)
- Data tools are live or expert-focused. f1-dash shows leaderboard, gaps, tyres and mini-sectors during sessions, MultiViewer requires F1 TV and is used even by teams, and f1-race-replay is a Python desktop app. — [f1-dash](https://f1-dash.com/); [iMore](https://www.imore.com/music-movies-tv/multiviewer-for-f1-the-fan-built-formula-1-app-so-popular-even-f1-teams-use-it); [GitTrend](https://gittrend.io/repo/IAmTomShaw/f1-race-replay)
- The closest post-race features are thin: Ferrari's "Race Timeline" (a list of key moments, single-team) and Box Box Club's "community race ratings". — [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-01-IBM-Debuts-New-AI-Powered-Features-for-the-Scuderia-Ferrari-App); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/DE/app/id1621241916)
- F1 Wordle-likes test career trivia (team, country, titles), not what happened in the last Grand Prix. — [Sportsdle](https://sportsdle.com/f1/wordle-maker)
- Teams are explicitly targeting "younger and newer" viewers with interactive features. Demand for casual-fan experiences is recognised, but it is served with generic quizzes and predictors. — [AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/ai-applications/ibm-ferrari-hp-unveil-formula-1-fan-engagement-app)

### Inferences
These are hypotheses for design, not verified facts. Candidate gaps Racing Simplified could own:
1. **Post-race "You Draw It" built on real timing data.** The fan predicts or draws something about the race just run, for example the gap trend between two drivers, when the undercut worked, or positions over laps. Then the true data is revealed with an explanation and a comparison with the crowd. No F1 product found does this.
2. **A shared daily or per-race puzzle built from that race's data, not career trivia.** For example: "Which driver had this lap-time trace?", or an Immaculate Grid-style grid of this race's facts, with rarity scoring and a spoiler-free share card (emoji or colour pattern). The Wordle and Immaculate Grid mechanics apply directly, and the F1 versions found are all career trivia.
3. **"Be the strategist" counterfactuals based on true data.** For example: you pit on lap X, and the real lap times and pit-loss show where you would have rejoined. Fantasy covers selection, not decisions within the race. Data tools show what happened, not what you would have done.
4. **The answer as a door into the recap.** Each answer should link into the race narrative, the way Immaculate Grid links to Baseball-Reference. That turns play into understanding, which is Racing Simplified's thesis.
5. **Saturation risks to avoid.** Generic predictors and quizzes (crowded, with teams already doing them), reaction tests (commodity) and season-long fantasy (dominated by F1 Fantasy at about 2M users).

### Gaps
- No direct evidence (surveys, Reddit threads) of what casual F1 fans say they miss after a race. This is inferred from product gaps only.
- No retention data (D1/D7) found for any F1 or sports interactive. The evidence on traction is about acquisition and virality, not retention.
- No evidence was found on whether post-race (versus pre-race) games perform worse or better in sport generally. This is a key risk to test.

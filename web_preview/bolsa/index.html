Paddock exchange

01 · The price

A driver is worth his place

Last place is worth 5. Every place he gains adds 5, so the leader is worth 110. Prices change at the end of every lap, with the real race.

02 · The trade

Buy low. Sell high.

Invest 100 in a driver when he is cheap. When he has climbed, cash out. You start with 1,000.

03 · The risk

If he retires, you lose it

A driver who retires is worth nothing, and so are your shares in him. Whatever you still hold at the flag is paid at his final place.

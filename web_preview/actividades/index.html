Call the race

The whole race replays in three minutes on the real Silverstone, 22 cars as lights. At the real turning points it freezes and asks what happens next; you stake points on how sure you are, then watch it happen. Same race, same calls for your friends.

The ghost

Pick a driver and run his strategy your way. Your calls build a ghost car, priced from this race's own lap times, tyres and stops, and it races the real field on the real track to the flag. Labelled as an estimate, always.

The ghost live

Pick a driver and the race runs without stopping. When his pit window opens the replay drops to slow motion and the BOX button is yours: call it when you see it. The tyre wears in front of you, the gaps move, and your ghost dives into the pit lane the moment you press. Each stop priced inside its own window, from this race's own tyres and stop cost.
